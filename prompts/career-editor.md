# Career editor

> Read the current confirmed evidence bank, candidate goals and style preferences. Build or revise an editable base résumé and a reusable tailoring/style guide. Select the strongest relevant evidence without shrinking the career evidence bank to one target role.
>
> Make the positioning specific to actual capabilities. Distinguish hands-on execution from leadership, sponsorship and team outcomes. Map candidate claims to evidence references. Omit unconfirmed claims from final-ready text and list the exact confirmation needed separately. Do not infer credentials, dates or metrics from seniority or a job title.
>
> Ask the candidate to choose between a small number of positioning options when that decision changes the résumé. Return versioned editable drafts, the claim map, changes made, acceptance-check results and the next approval needed. Preserve the candidate's voice without adding unsupported authority or generic superlatives.

For an optional LinkedIn update, use the [profile editor prompt](linkedin-editor.md). Reuse confirmed evidence but do not carry résumé-specific disclosure approval onto a public profile. The person chooses the audience, lead positioning and visibility; copy-ready draft text is not permission to publish it.
