Keep the whole career here: recent and older roles, projects, volunteering, leadership and learning. The résumé will select from this bank. Do not delete useful older evidence because it does not fit one role.

- Evidence status: [source-verified / candidate-confirmed / estimate / unresolved]
- Uncertainty / questions: [ ]
- Confidentiality and sharing permission: [ ]
- Relevant capabilities: [ ]
- Proposed wording: [ ]
- Candidate-approved wording / date / reviewer: [ ]

A leader can say they led a team outcome while crediting team execution. Do not turn “led” into “personally built.” If a metric has no reliable basis, remove the number and preserve the supported result. Estimates stay separate from final claims unless explicitly resolved and labeled appropriately.
