## A short consultative opening

**Avery:** “I'd like my LinkedIn to make more sense for operations and enablement roles. I have fifteen minutes. Can we start with the headline and About?”

**Agent:** “Yes. I'll reuse your confirmed evidence and the direct, calm voice we agreed on. I can review the text you choose to share and draft revisions here; I cannot see your live profile or its visuals, and nothing will be published. You can copy selected text yourself without connecting LinkedIn.”

Avery supplies this deliberately simple fictional snapshot: headline “Operations professional”; About “I work in operations.” The experience entry, skills, banner, photo, contact fields and settings are not supplied. The agent does not claim to have audited them or ask Avery to export everything.

The agent already has evidence-bank v1, style-guide v1 and search-brief v1. It reads those rather than repeating the career intake. The brief targets operations enablement and customer operations; its capabilities include onboarding process design, team leadership and cross-functional coordination. Those are capabilities and target directions, not proof that Avery has held every target title.

**Agent:** “Your confirmed examples make the handoff work more specific than the current headline. I'd lead with capabilities across both target families. An employer-first version could help if its name matters to your audience; an independent-project-first version would need a project we have not documented. May we use the E-001 and E-002 wording on a public profile, or should we keep either private?”

**Avery:** “Use those two examples and their supported capability terms publicly, without adding internal details. Capability-first works for me. Keep job-search signals and settings out of this pass.”

This fictional decision clears the two named claims for this public wording. It does not clear other résumé evidence, contact details, final draft text or a live edit. The agent carries those distinctions in the private packet.

## If Avery chooses agent-host execution later

Manual copying remains available. If Avery instead asks an authorized host to update a supported field, the agent follows the [execution guide](../../docs/linkedin-execution.md) and maintains separate profile execution JSON. The `python3 -m career_pipeline profile` commands initialize and inspect state, record events, list the next step, dispatch a scoped job once, and reconcile its result. The host must actually invoke its authorized integration or computer-use tool; the kit provides no live LinkedIn SDK or browser driver.

The selected headline would need exact fact, public, material and edit approvals tied to its field/content, plus current session/actor/account capability evidence and expiry. The host would observe the current headline, compare it with the expected pre-edit value, perform only the authorized write, then supply actual readback for reconciliation. A prepared or dispatched job alone would not change this example's publication state. An uncertain result would stop for inspection rather than trigger a duplicate write.

The automatic bridge supports only headline, About and stable-key experience body text. Skills are review/manual-only; title/date fields, unseen banner/photo, visibility, settings and notifications also remain outside automatic execution. Local record checks do not turn fictional consent or supplied observations into real consent or live evidence. This example authorizes and performs no external action.
